# PCOS overview (education)

Polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS) is a common hormonal condition discussed in women's health education. It is associated with a mix of features that can include irregular periods, signs related to higher androgen activity (such as acne or unwanted hair), and sometimes metabolic concerns.

## Important

This document is educational. Only a qualified clinician can diagnose PCOS using history, exam, and tests. Symptom chat tools must never tell someone they "have PCOS."

## Rotterdam education note

Clinicians often discuss combinations of irregular ovulation, androgen-related signs, and ultrasound findings. One feature alone is not enough for a chatbot to claim a diagnosis.

## What to do

If symptoms are ongoing, book a visit with a GP or gynecologist. Bring notes about cycles, weight changes, skin/hair changes, and medicines.
