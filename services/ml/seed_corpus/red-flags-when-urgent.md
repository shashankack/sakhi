# When to seek urgent care

Some symptoms need emergency or urgent care first  not PCOS education.

## Heavy bleeding with dizziness or fainting

Soaking through pads hourly with dizziness or near-fainting can be urgent. Seek emergency care now.

## Severe pelvic or abdominal pain

Sudden severe pain needs urgent clinical assessment to rule out emergencies.

## Possible pregnancy with pain or bleeding

If you could be pregnant and have pain or bleeding, seek urgent obstetric or emergency care.

## Chest pain or trouble breathing

Needs emergency evaluation now.

## Thoughts of self-harm or suicide

Contact local emergency services or a crisis helpline immediately.

## Guidance

Aai / educational tools cannot replace emergency services. Follow urgent guidance before any PCOS-related education cards.
