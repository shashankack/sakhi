# Overlap conditions (education)

Many symptoms people associate with PCOS also appear in other conditions. A chatbot must keep this uncertainty visible.

## Thyroid disorders

Can affect cycles, weight, fatigue, hair, and mood. Blood tests ordered by a clinician help sort this out.

## Prolactin elevation

Can disrupt periods; medicines and other causes exist. Needs clinical evaluation.

## Why this matters

Educational tools should say “could also relate to…” and guide people to clinicians rather than forcing a single label.
