# Hair thinning or hair fall

Noticeable shedding or thinning at the crown or parting can come from hormones, nutrient gaps, thyroid issues, stress (telogen effluvium), or genetics.

## PCOS education context

Some PCOS education materials mention female-pattern hair thinning related to androgens. Pattern and bloodwork matter more than a single symptom.

## Other possible causes

Iron deficiency, thyroid disease, postpartum shedding, and harsh styling also cause hair fall.

## Care pathway

A dermatologist or GP can assess pattern hair loss and decide on labs or referral.

## Questions for a clinician

- Does my hair pattern suggest a hormonal evaluation?
- Which blood tests should we start with?
- What treatments are evidence-based for my type of hair loss?
