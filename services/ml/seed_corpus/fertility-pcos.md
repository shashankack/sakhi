# Trying to conceive

Difficulty conceiving is common and emotionally heavy. Timing of ovulation, partner factors, age, and medical conditions all matter. Early supportive care is appropriate.

## PCOS education context

Irregular ovulation is one reason PCOS is discussed in fertility education. Many people with PCOS conceive with support; a clinician guides next steps from your full history.

## Other possible causes

Fallopian tube issues, sperm factors, endometriosis, thyroid disease, and unexplained infertility are other possibilities.

## Care pathway

See a gynecologist familiar with fertility evaluation. Bring cycle notes and any prior labs.

## Questions for a clinician

- How long should we try before further fertility tests?
- Do my cycle patterns suggest ovulation issues?
- What first-line evaluations do you recommend for us as a couple?
