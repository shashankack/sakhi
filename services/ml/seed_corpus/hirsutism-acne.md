# Facial hair and acne

New or thicker hair on the face, chin, chest, or abdomen, and persistent jawline acne, can feel distressing. Patterns vary by genetics and hormones; what matters is a change that is new for you.

## PCOS education context

Education about PCOS often mentions signs that can relate to higher androgen activity, including unwanted hair growth and acne. Appearance alone cannot confirm PCOS. Acne by itself is very common and has many causes.

## Other possible causes

Family hair patterns, cosmetics, steroids, stress, and other endocrine issues can drive these symptoms without PCOS.

## Self-care notes

- Photograph hair areas over weeks if useful for a clinician visit.
- Avoid harsh bleaching if skin is irritated; avoid picking acne to reduce scarring.
- Note if periods changed around the same time.

## Care pathway

A GP, gynecologist, or dermatologist can discuss evaluation and options.

## Questions for a clinician

- Is this hair or acne pattern something to investigate hormonally?
- What treatments are safe for me while we investigate?
- Should androgen-related blood tests be considered?
