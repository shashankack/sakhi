# Quick commerce essentials (future-ready)

Aai can **suggest comfort essentials** and open search pages on:

- [Blinkit](https://blinkit.com)
- [Zepto](https://www.zeptonow.com)
- [Swiggy Instamart](https://www.swiggy.com/instamart)

## What ships today

- Theme-aware suggestions (pads, heating pad, gentle wash, ORS, …)
- User taps an item → picks an app → browser/app opens **search**, not checkout
- Clear copy: education / convenience only  **no orders placed by Aai**

## Future partnership enhancement

- In-app cart handoff / affiliate deep-links with partner SDKs
- City/stock-aware availability
- Still never auto-buy medicines or claim treatment benefit

## Safety

- No prescription products as “treatment”
- Urgent flows still prioritize emergency care over shopping
