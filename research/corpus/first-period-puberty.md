# First period and puberty

## Plain-language explanation

The NHS says most girls start their periods when they are **about 12**, but they can start **as early as 8**.

Signs that a first period may be coming, according to the NHS:

- it is often **about 2 years after breasts start growing**
- underarm and pubic hair have grown

The NHS says it can take the body **some months** to get into a regular pattern. Once settled, periods typically come every 23 to 35 days and last 2 to 7 days.

## PCOS education context

The 2023 international guideline says irregular cycles are **normal in the first year** after periods start, as part of puberty. So irregular early periods alone do **not** suggest PCOS (now also called PMOS). See `adolescent-pcos.md`.

The NHS says acne can come with hormone changes during **puberty**.

This information is educational. It is not a diagnosis.

## Other possible causes and overlaps

The NHS lists puberty as a common cause of irregular periods. Other causes can include stress, big weight changes, exercising too much, and pregnancy. See `cycles-irregular.md`.

## Self-care notes

- The NHS says tampons, menstrual cups, disposable or reusable pads, and period underwear are **safe and suitable** for a first period. It suggests **pads** at first, since tampons and cups can take some getting used to. See `period-hygiene-products.md`.
- **Track your periods** on a calendar or app.
- Talk to a trusted adult, such as a parent, relative, teacher, or school nurse, with any questions.
- Parents: keep conversations calm and open, so a child feels safe sharing worries.

## Care pathway

- **Routine**: questions about a first period or what to use.
- **Soon**: the NHS advises seeing a GP if periods have **not started by age 15**, or **by 13 if there are no other signs of puberty**.
- **Urgent**: very heavy bleeding with feeling faint, or severe tummy pain. See `red-flags-when-urgent.md`.

Who to see: a **GP**, **paediatrician**, or **gynaecologist**.

## Questions for a clinician

- Is it normal that my periods have not started yet?
- Is it normal that my periods are not regular yet?
- How long might it take for my periods to settle?
- What period products are best for me to start with?
