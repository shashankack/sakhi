# Glossary and everyday phrases (English, Hindi, Hinglish)

## Plain-language explanation

People describe body changes in many different ways: in English, in Hindi, or in a mix of both (Hinglish). This glossary connects **everyday phrases** to the **topic** they usually relate to, and explains common medical words in simple language.

A phrase matching a topic only means the topic is **worth reading about**. It does **not** mean someone has a particular condition.

## PCOS education context

Many of the phrases below are commonly heard in conversations about PCOS (often called **PCOD** in India). The same phrases also apply to many other causes. Use them to find the right educational article, not to reach a conclusion.

This information is educational. It is not a diagnosis.

## Medical words explained

- **PCOS / PCOD**: polycystic ovary syndrome; a common hormone-related condition. In India, "PCOD" is often used to mean the same thing.
- **Menstrual cycle**: the time from the first day of one period to the first day of the next.
- **Ovulation**: when an ovary releases an egg, usually once per cycle.
- **Androgens**: hormones present in everyone; higher effects can cause extra hair, acne, or scalp thinning.
- **Hirsutism**: coarse, dark hair growing in a male-typical pattern on a woman's face or body.
- **Insulin resistance**: the body needs more insulin than usual to manage blood sugar.
- **Acanthosis nigricans**: dark, thick, velvety skin patches, often on the neck, underarms, or groin; linked with insulin resistance.
- **Prolactin**: a pituitary hormone involved in breast milk production; high levels can affect periods.
- **Thyroid (TSH)**: the thyroid gland controls metabolism; TSH is a common blood test for it.
- **Endometrium**: the lining of the womb that sheds during a period.
- **Ultrasound**: a painless scan using sound waves to see the ovaries and womb.
- **Gynaecologist**: a doctor for women's reproductive health.
- **Endocrinologist**: a doctor for hormone conditions.
- **Dermatologist**: a doctor for skin, hair, and nails.
