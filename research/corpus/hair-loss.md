# Hair fall and scalp hair thinning

## Plain-language explanation

The NHS says we can lose **between 50 and 100 hairs a day**, often without noticing. Hair fall becomes a concern when someone notices much more shedding than usual, or thinning that is getting worse.

The NHS says some hair loss is **permanent**, such as male and female pattern baldness, which usually runs in families. Other hair loss is **temporary** and can grow back.

## PCOS education context

WHO lists **hair thinning or female-pattern baldness** among the symptoms of PCOS. The NHS lists hair loss among the symptoms of PMOS (the new name for PCOS).

The 2023 international guideline says female-pattern hair loss **on its own** (without extra facial or body hair) is only a weak predictor of PCOS. So hair thinning alone does **not** suggest PCOS.

This information is educational. It is not a diagnosis.

## Other possible causes and overlaps

The NHS lists these temporary causes of hair loss:

- illness
- stress
- cancer treatment
- weight loss
- **iron deficiency**. See `anaemia-and-periods.md`.

The NHS also lists dry hair or hair loss as a symptom of **underactive thyroid**.

## Self-care notes

- The NHS says most hair loss does not need treatment, and temporary hair loss often grows back.
- The NHS advises speaking to a GP before considering a commercial hair clinic, and says no treatment is 100% effective.
- Eat a balanced diet that includes iron-rich foods. See `indian-diet-pcos.md`.
- If hair loss affects how you feel, tell your GP. The NHS says a GP may arrange counselling, and support groups can help.

## Care pathway

- **Routine**: the NHS advises seeing a GP if you are worried about hair loss. A GP may be able to find the cause by looking at your hair, and may check iron or thyroid levels.
- **Soon**: hair loss together with thick, dark hair appearing **suddenly** on the face or body, a lower voice, or bigger muscles (NHS: urgent GP appointment). See `hirsutism-acne.md`.
- A **dermatologist** can help with hair and scalp problems.

## Questions for a clinician

- What type of hair loss do I have?
- Should my iron, thyroid, or hormone levels be checked?
- Could a recent illness, stress, or weight loss explain the shedding?
- Could my hair thinning be related to my periods or other symptoms?
- What treatment options are there, and how long until I see results?
