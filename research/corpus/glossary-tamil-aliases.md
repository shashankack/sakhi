# Glossary and everyday phrases (Tamil and Tanglish)

## Plain-language explanation

Many people in Tamil Nadu and nearby regions describe body changes in **Tamil**, or in a mix of Tamil and English often called **Tanglish**. Many people type Tamil in English letters (romanised) instead of Tamil script.

This glossary connects **everyday Tamil phrases** to the **topic** they usually relate to. Each phrase has a plain-English meaning. It works the same way as `glossary-lay-aliases.md`, which covers English, Hindi, and Hinglish.

A phrase matching a topic only means the topic is **worth reading about**. It does **not** mean someone has a particular condition.

Spelling of romanised Tamil varies a lot (for example "vali" / "valli", "romba" / "rombha", "varala" / "varla"). The phrases here are common forms, not the only ones.

## PCOS education context

PCOS is often called **PCOD** in everyday speech, and also described as **"சினைப்பை நீர்க்கட்டி"** (sinaipai neerkatti, "water cysts in the ovary"). The condition now has a new name, **PMOS** (polyendocrine metabolic ovarian syndrome). See `pcod-vs-pcos.md`. The phrases below are often heard in conversations about PCOS, but they also apply to many other causes.

This information is educational. It is not a diagnosis.

## Phrase map by theme

### cycle_irregularity: irregular, late, or missing periods
- "period olunga varala": periods are not coming regularly
- "period late aagudhu": my period is coming late
- "period thalli pogudhu": my period keeps getting pushed back
- "rendu maasama period varala": no period for two months
- "maasa maasam varala": it does not come every month
- "மாதவிடாய் சரியாக வரவில்லை" (maadhavidai sariyaaga varavillai): periods are not coming properly
- "மாதவிலக்கு தள்ளிப் போகிறது" (maadhavilakku thallip pogiradhu): my period is getting delayed
- "இரண்டு மாதமாக வரவில்லை" (irandu maadhamaaga varavillai): it has not come for two months
- See: `cycles-irregular.md`, `adolescent-pcos.md`

### hirsutism: extra facial or body hair
- "mugathula mudi": hair on the face
- "thaadai la mudi": hair on the chin
- "meesai maadhiri mudi": hair like a moustache
- "udambula adhigama mudi": a lot of hair on the body
- "முகத்தில் முடி" (mugathil mudi): hair on the face
- "தாடையில் முடி" (thaadaiyil mudi): hair on the chin
- "மீசை போல முடி" (meesai pola mudi): moustache-like hair
- See: `hirsutism-acne.md`

### acne: pimples and breakouts
- "mugathula paru": pimples on the face
- "paru adhigama varudhu": I am getting a lot of pimples
- "pimple romba": too many pimples
- "skin romba oily": my skin is very oily
- "முகப்பரு" (mugapparu): pimples on the face
- "பருக்கள்" (parukkal): pimples
- See: `hirsutism-acne.md`

### weight_insulin: weight, hunger, blood sugar
- "weight romba eruthu": my weight is going up a lot
- "edai koodudhu": my weight is increasing
- "edai kuraiya maatenguthu": my weight will not come down
- "romba pasikudhu": I feel very hungry
- "inippu saapida aasai": I crave sweets
- "sugar irukku": I have been told my sugar is high
- "kazhuthula karuppu": dark skin on the neck
- "உடல் எடை கூடுகிறது" (udal edai koodugiradhu): body weight is increasing
- "எடை குறையவில்லை" (edai kuraiyavillai): weight is not reducing
- "கழுத்தில் கருப்பு" (kazhuthil karuppu): darkness on the neck
- See: `weight-insulin.md`, `diet-lifestyle-pcos.md`

### mood: mood changes, anxiety, low mood
- "mood sari illa": my mood is not okay
- "kovam varudhu": I get angry easily
- "manasu kashtama irukku": I feel sad or heavy-hearted
- "tension aa irukku": I feel tense or stressed
- "padhattama irukku": I feel anxious
- "azhugai varudhu": I feel like crying
- "மன அழுத்தம்" (mana azhutham): stress or mental pressure
- "கோபம் வருகிறது" (kobam varugiradhu): anger comes easily
- "மனசு சரியில்லை" (manasu sariyillai): my mind feels unwell
- See: `mood-fatigue.md`, `pms-pmdd.md`

### fatigue: tiredness and low energy
- "romba tired aa irukku": I feel very tired
- "sorvaa irukku": I feel worn out
- "udambula thembe illa": there is no strength in my body
- "eppavum thookam varudhu": I always feel sleepy
- "சோர்வு" (sorvu): tiredness
- "களைப்பு" (kalaippu): exhaustion
- "உடல் சோர்வு" (udal sorvu): body tiredness
- See: `mood-fatigue.md`, `heavy-periods.md`

### hair_loss: scalp hair thinning
- "mudi kottudhu": my hair is falling
- "mudi romba uthirudhu": a lot of hair is shedding
- "thalai mudi kuraiyudhu": the hair on my head is getting less
- "vazhukkai": a bald patch or baldness
- "முடி கொட்டுதல்" (mudi kottudhal): hair fall
- "முடி உதிர்தல்" (mudi udhirdhal): hair shedding
- See: `hair-loss.md`

### fertility_concerns: trying to conceive
- "kuzhandhai illa": we do not have a child yet
- "karbam aagala": I am not getting pregnant
- "baby try panrom": we are trying for a baby
- "kuzhandhai pirakkala": a baby has not come
- "karumuttai valaradhu illa": the egg is not growing
- "குழந்தை இல்லை" (kuzhandhai illai): no child
- "கர்ப்பம் ஆகவில்லை" (karbam aagavillai): not getting pregnant
- "கருமுட்டை" (karumuttai): egg (ovum)
- See: `fertility-pcos.md`, `pregnancy-and-pcos.md`

### Period pain and heavy bleeding (no theme id yet)
- "period la vayiru vali": belly pain during periods
- "idupu vali": pain in the lower back or waist
- "romba blood pogudhu": I am bleeding a lot
- "rattham kattiya varudhu": blood is coming in clots
- "மாதவிடாய் வலி" (maadhavidai vali): period pain
- "அதிக உதிரப்போக்கு" (adhiga udhirappokku): heavy bleeding
- See: `painful-periods.md`, `heavy-periods.md`, `endometriosis.md`

### Urgent phrases (always check red flags first)
- "romba blood pogudhu, mayakkam varudhu": bleeding a lot and feeling faint
- "vayiru romba vali, thaanga mudiyala": belly pain so bad I cannot bear it
- "karbama irukken, blood varudhu": I am pregnant and bleeding
- "saaganum pola irukku": I feel like dying
- "moochu vida mudiyala": I cannot breathe
- "nenju vali": chest pain
- "மயக்கம்" (mayakkam): fainting or dizziness
- "தாங்க முடியாத வலி" (thaanga mudiyaadha vali): unbearable pain
- "நெஞ்சு வலி" (nenju vali): chest pain
- "மூச்சு விட முடியவில்லை" (moochu vida mudiyavillai): cannot breathe
- See: `red-flags-when-urgent.md`

## Medical words explained

- **மாதவிடாய் / மாதவிலக்கு (maadhavidai / maadhavilakku)**: menstrual period.
- **சினைப்பை (sinaipai)**: ovary.
- **கருமுட்டை (karumuttai)**: egg (ovum).
- **நீர்க்கட்டி (neerkatti)**: a fluid-filled cyst. WHO says ovarian cysts are not required for a diagnosis of PCOS.
- **கருப்பை (karuppai)**: womb (uterus).
- **இரத்த சோகை (ratha sogai)**: anaemia, low haemoglobin.
- **தைராய்டு (thyroid)**: a gland in the neck; often called by the English word. A blood test (TSH) can check it.
- **சர்க்கரை நோய் (sarkkarai noi)**: diabetes; often just called "sugar".

## Other possible causes and overlaps

Every phrase above can come from many causes besides PCOS, including thyroid problems, high prolactin, pregnancy, stress, anaemia, and other gynaecological conditions. See `overlap-thyroid-prolactin.md`.

## Self-care notes

It is fine to describe symptoms to a clinician in Tamil or Tanglish. Writing down your own words, along with dates and how often things happen, helps the clinician understand.

## Care pathway

Use the care pathway in the topic file each phrase points to. Any **urgent phrase** above should lead to `red-flags-when-urgent.md` first. In India, dial **108** for an emergency ambulance. Mental health support is **Tele-MANAS 14416** or **1800-89-14416** (toll-free, 24x7).

See `care-pathways.md` for who to see and how soon.

## Questions for a clinician

- What is the medical word for what I am describing?
- Could you explain my test results in simple words, or in Tamil?
- Is there a Tamil-speaking doctor or nurse I can talk to?
