# Copy & safety design

## Allowed

- “These symptoms are commonly discussed in the context of PCOS education.”
- “This is not a diagnosis.”
- “Other causes can include thyroid issues, stress, …”
- “Consider talking to a GP or gynecologist.”

## Forbidden

- “You have PCOS.”
- “You are diagnosed with …”
- Prescribing medicines or dosages
- Guaranteeing lab results or treatment outcomes

## Urgency copy

When red flags fire: lead with emergency/urgent care guidance; PCOS education is secondary or hidden until safe.
